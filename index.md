An instrument for listening sessions, in the EVP and spirit box tradition.

The room makes a noise. Somewhere in it, now and then, something that sounds like a voice. What
that is has been argued about for sixty years and this app does not settle it. It does two
things no other app of its kind does: the room sets the rhythm, and what you record is honest.

*by Altrove Labs · for Android*

## The sensors choose the syllables

At the instant of each phrase, Aftervoice reads the phone's magnetometer, accelerometer and
gyroscope. The three values are rounded, hashed together with the session's own seed, and the
syllables come out of that number. A tenth of a microtesla of difference in the magnetic field
is a different phrase.

That is worth being precise about, because most apps in this genre wave at their sensors. Here
the sensors are not colouring a rhythm around a random generator: they are inside the choice of
the sounds themselves. Separately, how restless the room is decides how often the voices come
and how long they are — and a phone that gets picked up is asked to be still again, which is
what the twenty seconds of calibration at the start of a session are for.

What the app will not do is tell you what makes the field move. It measures what the sensors
measure, and stops there.

## What you record is honest

Press Registra and the app writes two separate tracks: what it made, exactly as you heard it,
and the phone's microphone, which is the room. Play either alone or both together. Mark
moments while you listen, name them afterwards, share the file.

The app's own audio carries a [machine-readable mark](detector.html) that says it is synthetic —
in the sound itself, so it survives sharing and re-encoding. Anyone can check it, with a
detector we publish and do not control the answer of.

Nothing else about a session is interpreted for you. The app never shows you a word while you
are listening: what you hear is yours to hear.

## The session stays on the phone

No accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking. Recordings, the microphone, diary, notes,
bookmarks and sensor data are never uploaded. Only a generated-audio report you explicitly
submit sends its text and minimal technical details to Altrove Labs; it never sends audio.

The full [privacy policy](privacy/it.html) says it in detail, and every sentence in it
corresponds to something checkable in the app: the report payload, a backup rule that excludes
the diary, and a folder that is private to the app.

## Practical

- Six interface languages and two voice languages, chosen separately.
- The voice bank for a language downloads once, about 120 to 140 MB, with the size said first.
- Headphones, dark room, low volume. That is the whole instruction.
- No ads, no subscription, no account.
